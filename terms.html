In plain English
Play fairly, respect other people, and use Pippo for entertainment. Its coins are game points. The developer can update features as the bot grows.

Who can use Pippo

You must meet Discord’s minimum age requirements and any additional requirements where you live. Use of Pippo also requires compliance with Discord’s Terms of Service, its Community Guidelines, and the rules of the server where you use the bot. If you are not old enough to agree to these terms yourself, a parent or legal guardian must agree on your behalf where required by law.

You may add or configure Pippo in a server only when you have permission to do so. Server administrators remain responsible for their server’s permissions and moderation.

Play fairly and respect others

Do not use Pippo for harassment, threats, hateful conduct, illegal activity, or sharing content you do not have the right to use. Do not submit another person’s confidential information or credentials. Do not spam commands, exploit bugs, manipulate scores or coin balances, or interfere with the bot or other players.

Commands that mention, pair, or interact with other members should be used respectfully. Stop if another person asks you to stop. Compatibility scores, character assignments, FLAMES results, and similar responses are playful entertainment; they are not factual judgments about people or relationships.

Games, content, and AI results

Pippo provides games and entertainment. Trivia, movie data, external definitions, images, and generated responses can be incomplete, outdated, or incorrect. Community-written definitions may include mature language. Choose features appropriate for your server and its members.

AI judgments are for entertainment and may be inaccurate or unexpected. If AI judging is unavailable, Pippo uses a clearly labelled random fallback.

You retain any rights you hold in content you submit. You permit Pippo to process and display that content as needed to carry out the command or game you requested. Do not submit material that infringes someone else’s rights.
