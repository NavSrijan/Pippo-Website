🎮Start here

/play

Your next game starts here.

Browse the game menu by category, then choose solo play or invite your friends.

.play
🎬Movies

/bollywood

Nine tries. One familiar film.

Guess the Bollywood title one letter at a time, with cast reveals, random letters, and summaries to help. Start the command, then choose Play solo or Play with friends.

.bollywood
🎥Movies

/hollywood

Take your shot at Tinseltown.

Guess a Hollywood movie letter by letter, use weighted hints, or risk a full-title shot for bonus points. Start the command, then choose Play solo or Play with friends.

.hollywood
📜Movies

/plotster

A movie mystery with a price on every clue.

Solve movie titles from a keyword ladder. Wrong guesses reveal more clues; cast, film details, and plot hints trade points for help. Play solo or with the channel. Start the command, then choose Play solo or Play with friends.

.plotster
🧠Movies

/quizster

Movie trivia. Beat the clock. Build a streak.

Play Bollywood, Hollywood, or mixed movie trivia. Earn streak bonuses, spend shared hints, and vote to skip a round. Start the command, then choose Play solo or Play with friends.

.quizster
🖼️Movies

/poster

Name the movie before the poster clears.

Choose Bollywood, Hollywood, or Mix. Race to identify each pixelated movie poster; every guess gets an immediate response, early answers score more, and a three-title streak can restore a team life. Start the command, then choose Play solo or Play with friends.

.poster
🎱Party games

/bingo

Classroom Bingo, with Pippo in the mix.

Play the familiar 1–25 classroom game. Choose a number from your ticket on your turn; everyone marks it if they have it. First to five lines wins. Start the command, then choose Play solo or Play with friends.

.bingo
❎Party games

/tictactoe

Challenge Pippo or a friend.

Play classic Tic Tac Toe with a friend or choose Pippo's easy, normal, or perfect hard mode. Start the command, then choose Play solo or Play with friends.

.tictactoe
🃏Party games

/memory

One more flip.

Find eight pairs solo for a move-based star rating, or challenge a friend. Matches keep your turn; misses pass it. Start the command, then choose Play solo or Play with friends.

.memory
🌈Party games

/flood

Capture the rainbow.

Pick colours to absorb connected tiles. Race past 50% of the board; Pippo chooses the move that gains the most territory. Start the command, then choose Play solo or Play with friends.

.flood
⚔️Party games

/clash

A toaster versus a time traveller?

Pit your wildest creation against a friend or Pippo. The AI judge explains its verdict; offline random results are clearly labelled. Start the command, then choose Play solo or Play with friends.

.clash
✏️Party games

/npat

Name. Place. Animal. Thing.

Race through five 45-second rounds with up to five friends. Lock in four answers before anyone else does: unique answers score more, shared answers still count, and a perfect card earns a bonus. Start the command, then choose Play solo or Play with friends.

.npat
✍️Fun & social

/onewordstory

A story nobody could plan.

Build a story in rotating one-word turns, then try surprise twist cards for bonus points. Play with up to five friends or write solo. Start the command, then choose Play solo or Play with friends.

.onewordstory
💕Fun & social

/ship

Let Pippo play matchmaker.

Get a playful compatibility reading for two names or server members. Reroll the same pair up to three times for a fresh Pippo verdict; just for fun.

.ship Pippo Coffee
🔥Fun & social

/flames

A little school-day nostalgia.

Play classic FLAMES with a letter-by-letter reveal and the original outcome images.

.flames Pippo Coffee
🛰️Fun & social

/laser

Incoming: one dramatic laser.

Watch the classic slow emoji laser strike build through edits to one message, then hit its target.

.laser @friend
📺Fun & social

/cartoon

A familiar face from a favourite show.

Draw a random cartoon character and spin up to five more times. Each pick shows the character, their show, and randomized Pippo cartoon-show art.

.cartoon
✨Fun & social

/cartoonme

Meet your cartoon alter ego.

See your assigned character or check a friend. Choose a different show and character in the shop for 1,000 coins.

.cartoonme
🎱Fun & social

/8ball

Four shakes. One question.

Ask Pippo's Magic 8-Ball, then use up to three owner-only shakes to try another answer. Just for fun.

.8ball "Will we win movie night?"
📖Fun & social

/what

One word. Twelve possible rabbit holes.

Browse up to 12 unique Urban Dictionary definitions, with examples, contributors, and community vote totals. Entries are community-written and may contain mature language.

.what "movie night"
💋Fun & social

/kiss

Send a greeting. Let them choose.

Offer a playful kiss request that the recipient can accept or pass. No target? Pippo sends a friendly air-kiss instead.

.kiss @friend
🐶Animals

/dog

Pup Parade: four good dogs, one button.

Browse four unique dog photos in one invoker-only carousel. Each photo gets a readable Pippo caption, with rotating Pippo dog-park art alongside.

.dog
🐱Animals

/cat

Cat Parade: four curious kitties.

Browse four unique cat photos in one invoker-only carousel. Each photo gets a readable Pippo caption, with rotating Pippo cat-scene art alongside.

.cat
🐕Animals

/woof

Five dog photos. One shared leaderboard.

Play five dog-breed photo rounds. In friends mode, the whole chat can guess and score points; in solo mode, only you play. Wrong choices are eliminated on the game post. Choose Play solo or Play with friends to start.

.woof
🐈Animals

/meow

Five cat photos. One shared leaderboard.

Play five cat-breed photo rounds. In friends mode, the whole chat can guess and score points; in solo mode, only you play. Wrong choices are eliminated on the game post. Choose Play solo or Play with friends to start.

.meow
🐾Animals

/breedme

Your canine counterpart.

Get a stable, playful dog-breed match for yourself or a friend, with that breed's temperament and typical lifespan alongside a captioned photo.

.breedme
😺Animals

/meowme

Find your inner cat.

Get a stable cat-breed match for yourself or a friend, with temperament, typical lifespan, and a captioned breed photo.

.meowme
🪙Coins & shop

/balance

Count your bragging rights.

Check your current virtual Pippo coin balance.

.balance
🎁Coins & shop

/send_coins

Share a little of your treasure.

Transfer virtual Pippo coins to another member. Self-transfers, bot recipients, non-positive amounts, and amounts above the database limit are rejected.

.send_coins @friend 10
🛍️Coins & shop

/shop

Spend your game-night earnings.

Open Pippo's shop to find available coin purchases, including cartoon character customisation.

.shop
🎭Coins & shop

/shop character

Choose your own cartoon identity.

Browse shows and characters, then spend 1,000 virtual coins to choose the identity shown by cartoonme. The price is shown before selection, and the purchase saves atomically.

.shop character
🏆Utilities

/leaderboard

Who has the bragging rights?

Browse shared Bollywood/Hollywood hangman and Poster scores, Plotster scores, or coin rankings. Movie game scores remain combined because the existing records do not distinguish those modes.

.leaderboard
👋Start here

/help

Find your way around Pippo.

Browse interactive help by category, or ask for the usage and description of a specific command.

.help
🏓Utilities

/ping

Pippo, are you there?

Check that Pippo is online and see both its Discord gateway latency and command response time.

.ping
⚙️Utilities

/change_prefix

Make Pippo speak your server’s language.

Members with Manage Server can set a short prefix without spaces, then use `reset` to restore the default dot.

.change_prefix ! or .change_prefix reset
💡Utilities

/suggest

Have an idea for the next round?

Send a private suggestion or bug report to Pippo’s developer using the slash-command feedback form.

/suggest
🌤️Utilities

/signup

A friendlier start to the day.

Open the greeting preferences and choose the times Pippo can send you an optional daily DM.

.signup greetings
☀️Utilities

/signup greetings

Pick the greetings you want.

Choose morning, afternoon, evening, or night DMs (scheduled in India time). Submitting all options off unsubscribes you from every greeting.

.signup greetings true false false false
